How to read a sheet

Left: tight zoom with the boxes. Right: 600 px of context, where the red cross is the catalog's nominal pixel. The catalog position is interpolated from the frame footprint and can be more than a thousand pixels off on LROC NAC crops, so the distance between the cross and the box is itself a measurement.
