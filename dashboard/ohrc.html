Natural terrain · training

Chandrayaan-2 OHRC tiles

1024 × 1024 tiles cut from ISRO's Orbiter High Resolution Camera at 0.23–0.26 m per pixel, the sharpest lunar orbital imagery there is. Three calibrated scenes of cratered southern highlands; no catalogued hardware lies within 140 km of any of them.

—Source · data/ohrc-set/metadata.csv · HF natural-terrain_training/moon_chandrayaan2-ohrc
01

Scenes

Each scene is a 12,000 × 93,693 px strip (3 km × 22 km). Tiles are spread evenly along the strip; tiles that are mostly saturated, black or featureless are skipped. Click a tile to see it at full resolution. The split is fixed per scene by a hash of the product id, as in every other Xenarch set.
